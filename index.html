    GET STARTED

    Three simple steps to build a habit

    01

    Plan your month

    Set a budget based on the allowance or money you have available. You can save a different budget for each month.

    02

    Note the little things

    Add your expense, amount, category, and date. Recording even small purchases helps you see the whole picture.

    03

    Review and adjust

    Check your remaining budget and category totals. Use the month selector to review earlier spending and plan your next month.

    A LITTLE EVERYDAY GUIDANCE

    Make your allowance work for you

    Start with essentials

    Leave room for transport, meals, and school materials before planning optional purchases.

    Check in once a week

    A quick review can help you notice repeated spending, such as snacks or extra trips, while there is still time to adjust.

    Keep your budget realistic

    Use what you actually spend as a starting point. If a month goes over budget, review the entries and make a practical plan for the next one.

    ABOUT POCKET NOTES

    A simple tracker for student life

    Pocket Notes is a practice project designed to make everyday expense tracking easy to understand. It tracks spending in Philippine pesos across Food, Transport, School, Personal, and Other categories.

    You enter expenses yourself, so your totals reflect the entries you record. The category filter changes the list; the monthly summary always includes every expense in that month.

    Where are my entries saved?

    Your expenses and budgets are saved in this browser on this device. Refreshing keeps them available, but clearing browser data removes them. There is no account, cloud sync, or automatic backup.

    Made a mistake?

    Delete the incorrect entry and add it again with the correct details. You’ll be asked to confirm before an expense is deleted.
